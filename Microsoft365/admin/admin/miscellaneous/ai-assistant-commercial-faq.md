# AI assistant for commercial products FAQ

## Summary

The frequently asked questions (FAQ) in this article are about the AI assistant that helps administrators of Microsoft commercial products with support questions. They explain what the AI assistant is designed to do, its limitations, and how your chat data is collected, used and retained.

## What is the AI assistant?

The AI assistant is a chat experience in the support workflow for Microsoft commercial products. It helps administrators find answers to support questions before or instead of opening a support case.

## What can the AI assistant do?

The AI assistant can:

- Ask clarifying questions to understand your issue.
- Search approved Microsoft support guidance and documentation for relevant information.
- Summarize troubleshooting steps and show the sources it used so you can check them.

## What can't the AI assistant do?

The AI assistant only answers questions. It can't:

- Create, update or close support cases.
- View or change settings, users, licenses or data in your tenant.
- Run commands, scripts or diagnostics on your behalf.
- Take actions in other systems based on what you type or paste into the chat.

It isn't designed for consumer (personal) Microsoft accounts.

## What are the limitations of the AI assistant?

- **Answers can be wrong or incomplete.** AI-generated answers can contain mistakes, missteps, or not apply to your configuration. Always check the linked sources before you make changes to your tenant.
- **Sources can be imperfect.** The assistant might link an article that's only partly relevant. Check that the source matches your scenario.
- **No access to your tenant.** The assistant can't see your configuration, so its answers are general guidance.
- **English only.** The assistant experience is available only in the English language.

## How can I get help from a person?

To contact a support professional, select the blue **Contact Support** button at the bottom of the support panel. The button becomes available after your first question and the AI assistant's response to it. Use the button to open the standard support case creation experience.

## What data does the AI assistant collect?

The AI assistant collects the questions you type, the content you paste, and the AI assistant's responses. If you choose to give feedback such as a rating and optional comments, that feedback is also collected.

## How is my chat data used?

Chat data is used to provide the conversation, to monitor the AI assistant for safety and quality issues, and to evaluate and improve the AI assistant. Your chat data isn't used to train the underlying AI models.

## How long is my chat data kept?

- Conversation records used to monitor and improve the AI assistant are kept for 30 days. Before they're stored, personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, credit card numbers and other account identifiers is replaced with placeholders.
- Your chat history is available to you for 30 days.
- Feedback is stored for 30 days, and your personal data in feedback comments is redacted.
