# Cannot disable lock PIN on Yealink MP54 and MP56 phones

## Summary

This article explains why you can't disable the device lock PIN on Yealink MP54 and MP56 phones in Microsoft Teams.

## Symptoms

On a Yealink MP54 or MP56 phone, you try to use a configuration profile or device settings to prevent users from setting a device lock PIN. However, the phone still allows or requires the lock PIN setting.

## Cause

This behavior is by design. On Yealink MP54 and MP56 phones, the device lock PIN can't be disabled.

## Resolution

There isn't a supported way to disable the device lock PIN on these phones.
